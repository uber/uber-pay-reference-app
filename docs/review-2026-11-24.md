ordered operator re-drive, Retry-After, account injection, key conflicts,
published example/errata, return queries, poison completion, bidirectional payouts,
stale-generation fencing and uncertain202/503/409 processor responses, read

actual ledger deltas and lookup after crash/lost response; asynchronous/deposit/

and a commit-boundary barrier;100000-row partial-index plans; and bounded public
admission alongside signed money calls. These are correctness/abuse regressions,
not a production throughput benchmark or every-possible-combination claim.
